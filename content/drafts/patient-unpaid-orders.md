# Paying an existing Rupa order
Proposed demo revision—not Fullscript-approved.

## Paying an existing unpaid order

If your provider has not transferred their Rupa data and you have an existing unpaid Rupa order, pay it by December 23, 2026.
Under that condition, unpaid orders are canceled after that date.
This conditional guidance does not verify whether your provider has transferred your data or whether your order is unpaid.

## Applicability

This draft covers existing unpaid orders only, not new orders or account-specific status.
Its date routing includes December 23 and excludes December 24 onward; it does not establish a cutover hour or timezone.
Later payment or recovery instructions are not supplied by this passage.
